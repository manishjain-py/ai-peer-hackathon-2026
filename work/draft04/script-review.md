# Draft 04 — independent opening script review

Reviewer: creative_review_04. Date: 2026-09-18.

Scope: the 13 new opening lines in `video/public/draft04/script.json`, their scene
windows, and the bridge into the retained personal brief. The unchanged Draft 03
dialogue was not re-reviewed. This review does not approve rendered motion or
voice delivery.

## Decision

**The opening is ready for voice production.** It answers the latest user request
in the right order: a person under divided attention, emotional release, creation,
company profile, work context, tool access, then useful personal relevance.

The specific counts are narrated as well as displayed. This is more literal than
my original suggestion, but reasonable because those examples are the user's
central request. Avoid adding further spoken inventory. Let the source-change
and opportunity details be quiet visual foreshadowing.

## Recommended small refinements

| Line | Recommendation | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `intro_sourcegraph` | “Sourcegraph finds your services, and the code behind them.” | “Finds” describes learning more directly than “connects” and gives Sourcegraph a clear role. |
| `intro_github` | If the take feels rushed: “GitHub adds your code and recent changes.” | The original repositories phrase is syllable-heavy for 2.95 seconds. Show the team-owned repository visually. |
| `intro_access` | If the take exceeds 5.3 seconds: “Choose Slack and Zoom access. Set its limits, and when to ask you.” | Preserve understandable permissions and human judgment without speeding up speech. |

`intro_day` is the shortest slot at 1.05 seconds. It can work as a crisp three-word
line but should not be forced to fit by aggressive speed-up. The next line starts
at 1.65 seconds, leaving a small amount of recovery if needed. The natural
1.2-second gap between the stated limit of `intro_focus` and the next start is
valuable: keep it as the overload-to-relief release.

## Meaning and claim checks

- “Start with one click” means starting the process; the subsequent source and
  access steps make it clear that one click is not the whole setup.
- A One Airbnb profile supplies the role/team/project starting point, rather
  than claiming that it contains every detail about Manish.
- Sourcegraph and GitHub have complementary context roles. Retain one visible
  Chargebacks node across them to make the connection comprehensible.
- Configuration precedes action. “Set what it can answer, and when to ask you”
  supports the later Zoom decision boundary.
- The setup must retain a visible illustrative-data concept label and use authored
  profiles/source content, not suggest these are live internal screenshots.
- Email, company events, open checks and the saved handoff idea need supporting
  visual sources because the following brief relies on them. Root has confirmed
  these are in the setup design.
- “Meet AI Peer. So you can focus where you're needed most.” addresses the
  user's latest attention problem clearly. The retained brief demonstrates both
  current work and opportunities, keeping the promise wider than routine replies.

## Creative conditions that still require viewing

The words alone cannot deliver the user's requested improvement. Before final
approval, inspect whether the figure actually tracks and reacts to demands,
whether peak counts/invites are readable, whether the ring visibly turns into
support, and whether setup gives the audience time to understand each extraction.
The decisive standard is emotional and explanatory clarity at normal speed,
not merely no clipping and valid timing.
