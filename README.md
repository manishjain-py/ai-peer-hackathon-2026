# AI Peer

**An AI teammate that knows your work and keeps it moving.**

AI Peer learns an employee's projects, code, docs, conversations and meetings, keeps
them aware of what matters, and covers for them when they are away: it answers
colleagues with sources, attends a meeting as a clearly labelled AI, and briefs them
afterwards. Built for Airbnb's hackathon (16 to 20 Nov 2026), generic first, with Learn
Like Magic as the test company.
