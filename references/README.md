## What is confirmed

[BUCK's case study](https://buck.co/work/notion-ai) describes cel animation and
Rive's State Machine for the Notion assistant's interactive character behavior.
That establishes the **mascot** workflow. It does not establish which application
edited or composited the complete launch film.

[Greyduck's case study](https://www.greyduck.tv/case-studies/slack-canvas) describes
its Canvas launch work across motion design, voiceover, sound design, and audio
mixing. Its studio upload is a closely matching 86-second Canvas film; use the
research's version caveat when attributing an exact cut.

**The complete reference films' editing applications and exact music tracks
remain unknown.** Suggestions such as After Effects, coded animation, or a
specific rendering stack are reproduction approaches, not verified credits.

## Audio evidence boundary

The inherited analysis used browser-captured mono audio, local faster-whisper
ASR/voice activity detection, RMS measurements, and exploratory beat tracking.
Slack yielded coherent narration; Notion yielded no detected speech despite an
active soundtrack. The analysis did not include direct model listening and did
not identify exact instruments, sound effects, music tracks, or original master
loudness. Automatic tempo estimates were too variable to claim exact BPM.

## Apply the references

Use Slack's explanatory continuity and Notion's visual economy. Build original
AI Peer interfaces and transitions; choose new appropriately licensed audio.
Preserve a clear separation between source observations, inferred techniques,
and our own production choices. The references are for study, not asset reuse.
