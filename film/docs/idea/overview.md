# Product overview

**Status: proposed product vision.** The current deliverable is a concept film;
the live product is not implemented. This document interprets the
[complete original idea](../../source-material/ai-peer-original.txt).

## One-sentence pitch

AI Peer is a personal AI teammate that understands an employee’s work, keeps
them current on relevant changes and opportunities, prepares useful next steps,
and helps colleagues within delegated limits when the employee is unavailable.

## The problem worth showing

The employee’s attention is finite. Repeated routine questions, unread email,
meeting demands and scattered updates pull focus away from work that needs
their judgment. Manish’s latest direction makes this divided attention visible:
the Peer supports routine work and personal awareness so the employee can choose
where their focus belongs.

Work context is spread across documents, chat, meetings, repositories, and the
people who connect those pieces. An engineer can spend more time waiting for
a colleague's clarification than doing the technical work itself. Across India
and U.S. time zones, one additional question can push a simple dependency into
another working day.

The value runs in two directions: **help the employee know what matters and
what to do next**, and **help colleagues get useful context without waiting**.
A service change, a relevant event or a fitting opportunity can matter even when
no one directly mentions the employee. The original source’s delay examples
are illustrative, not measured results.

Manish’s 18 September clarification makes personal awareness a central product
use case. A daily brief should connect permitted email, Slack and company-event
signals to the employee’s actual services, projects, open questions and saved
interests. Each item should explain its relevance and offer a useful next step.

## Intended experience

1. **Create a Peer.** Start with the employee’s profile and permitted sources;
   connect their team/projects to work, repositories and services. Draft 07
   illustrates OneAirbnb, GitHub, and a proposed AI Peer plugin for AirChat local
   work, followed by Slack/Zoom and selected Gmail/calendar choices.
   These source names are a proposed setup path, not verified live connectors.
2. **Learn relevant work.** Discover projects, documents, conversations, services
   and collaborators. Include versioned code and approved local work in progress
   through the proposed AirChat plugin, while keeping their states distinct.
3. **Stay current.** Track changes with evidence, source timestamps, and conflicts.
4. **Keep the employee a step ahead.** Surface relevant changes and opportunities,
   explain why each matters, prioritize what needs attention, and prepare work for review.
5. **Represent within limits.** Supply routine context while clearly identifying as AI.
6. **Return a handoff.** Show what happened, what evidence was used, and what needs review.

## What makes the proposal distinctive

The product combines personal relevance, source-grounded work context, and
permissioned representation. It would know why a project matters to a particular
employee and what that employee is allowed to delegate. It would carry a
conversation forward and leave an accountable record for the employee.

These are proposed product characteristics, not a claim that no other product
offers overlapping capabilities. Competitive differentiation remains to be tested.

## Human role

The Peer extends the employee's availability for routine context. The employee
remains responsible for consequential decisions, corrections, and delegation.
The concept should never suggest that a person must work continuously because
their Peer is available continuously.

## Initial audience and story

The current 4:23 film follows Manish in Risk Partner Systems. It begins
with a happy seated employee interrupted by notifications, then introduces and sets up
his Peer. Four clear chapters show a missed BOBO launch connection, a Synthetic
Cancellation clarification across time zones, delegated Zoom context and missed
notifications recovered with source previews and useful actions. A daily brief
brings people, opportunities and service dependencies together.

Professional names and work context come from Manish's supplied file and
reported experiences. Invitations, messages, interface states and AI actions
are authored scenarios; they do not imply connected internal systems.
