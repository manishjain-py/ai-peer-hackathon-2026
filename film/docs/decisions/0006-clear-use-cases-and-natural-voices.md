# 0006 — Clear use-case chapters and natural voices

Date: **2026-09-19**. Status: implemented in the concept film, reviewed and delivered as Draft 06.

## Decision

Incorporate Manish's completed feedback round into a four-minute concept film.
Use a full-screen title before each of four chapters: proactive BOBO awareness,
clarification across time zones, delegated Zoom context, and missed notifications.
Show the situation, Peer contribution and practical result in each chapter.

Introduce the employee as happy and energetic, arriving, sitting and beginning
work before notifications visibly change their expression and posture. Keep the
attention circle, explicit creation flow, OneAirbnb/GitHub/AirChat context and
clear Peer identity. End with “More room for your best work” and acknowledge the
broader possibilities after demonstrating specific examples.

Replace all previous speech with carefully cast ElevenLabs performances using
an ElevenLabs credential the user already had locally, without exposing or copying it.
Select restrained narration and conversational Peer/team voices. Keep native
speech speed and adjust timing instead of speeding up clips. Preserve the
original upbeat musical palette.

## Why

The user wants clear impacts, believable human performances and a wider view of
personal value. Real BOBO and clarification experiences communicate relevance
and collaboration more concretely than generic status updates. Appreciation,
relevant invitations and platform changes make the personal brief useful to
Manish himself. Title cards help viewers distinguish the examples.

## Boundaries and tradeoffs

User-reported facts, proposed AI actions and illustrative events remain distinct.
Use the purchased item's expiry from the supplied context, without inventing an
exact data contract. The BOBO contact is a role, not an invented actual owner.
Invitation acceptance follows a visible choice; Zoom launch approval stays with
Manish. No external company message or calendar action occurs during production.

The four-minute length gives each impact room to read. The survey example is
preserved as an alternative in the feedback record; Cassette is the featured
missed Slack notification. Earlier drafts stay intact. This record supersedes
the voice and chapter structure of earlier production decisions, not their facts.
