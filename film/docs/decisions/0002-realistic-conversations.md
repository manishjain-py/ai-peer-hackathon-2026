# 0002 — Realistic Slack and Zoom conversations

Date: 2026-09-18. Supersedes the approximately two-minute duration and abstract
meeting/chat presentation of [the first film decision](0001-project-and-first-film.md).

## Direction

Manish requested realistic Slack and Zoom screenshots, fuller conversations,
a stronger advertising-style opening, smoother animation and independent creative
reviews of script and video. A three-to-four-minute film is acceptable.

Draft 02 uses one connected Chargebacks scenario. A night-time Slack exchange
supplies the use case, and Sarah’s answer becomes fresh evidence in the subsequent
Zoom meeting. The meeting explicitly asks for absent Manish, then the identified
Peer contributes his project status. It does not approve the launch date.

## Production decisions

Use authored, recognizable app reconstructions and generated fictional webcam
stills so the UI can animate smoothly and all content is controlled. This is a
concept film, not a recording of functional Slack or Zoom integrations. Do not
claim the webcam stills are live or lip-synced footage.

Preserve Draft 01. Keep the 240-second source timeline; apply a shared 15.9-second
editorial trim map to speech-free Zoom gaps for a 224.1-second final film. Keep
all spoken words and verify the final picture/audio alignment.

## Review changes

Independent review corrected status continuity, simplified jargon, required a
visible source excerpt, improved message framing, and tightened meeting rhythm.
Sarah offers test examples; the handoff must not imply those tests were completed.

messages, source documents and outcomes are illustrative. Consequential decisions
remain with the employee. See [Draft 02 storyboard](../video/draft-02-storyboard.md).
