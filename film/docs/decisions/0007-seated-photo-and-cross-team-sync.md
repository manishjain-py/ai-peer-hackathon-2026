# 0007 — Seated photographic opening and clearer cross-team sync

Date: **2026-09-19**. Status: implemented in the concept film, verified and delivered as Draft 07.

Manish found the walking/sitting cartoon unprofessional and asked to drop that
motion in favor of an already seated, happy employee reacting to notifications.
He also requested generic Zoom participants and a clearer cross-team meeting
scenario grounded in real Risk Partner Systems work.

## Decision

Use a fictional photographic employee with a locked seated composition and
restrained expression transitions. Remove the walk and articulated body animation.
Keep the attention circle and happy-to-worried-to-relieved emotional arc.

Rebuild Zoom around Product, D-REX, Case Platform and RPS. Generic participants
Maya, Alex and Jordan notice the absent RPS employee; the explicitly identified
Peer represents Manish, shares supported status, asks for a fresh product-level
case, hears partner responses and saves their follow-ups. Manish retains review
of validation and launch. Give the scene 49 seconds so the exchange is clear.
The resulting film is 4:23; other content is retained with a later time offset.

Use the existing ElevenLabs cast and exact unchanged performances. Add a distinct
host voice for Maya. Do not regenerate or rewrite unrelated scenes.

## Review requirements

Photographic transitions must not hold blended or doubled eyes. Keep complete
expression states; inspect short transitions and relief routing independently.
Meeting notes must follow the relevant spoken clause or turn. Merged data changes
do not prove rollout, and offers to coordinate/trace cases are pending actions.
The people and dialogue are fictional, while project context retains its supplied
source date. This remains a concept film, not an implemented meeting delegate.
