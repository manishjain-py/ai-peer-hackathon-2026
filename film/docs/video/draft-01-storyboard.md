# Draft 01 — AI Peer

Status: first concept film; all interface states and example data are illustrative. No live product integration is represented as implemented. Working name: AI Peer. Target: 120 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps.

The story follows Manish and one cross-time-zone dependency. The Peer learns his work, stays current, explains a requirement while he is offline, helps in a meeting, and gives him an auditable handoff. The employee retains important decisions.

## Creative direction

Generous warm-white space; dark navy for offline tension; coral for product personality; mint for useful outcomes. Large, readable interface text. Use the references' connected UI movement and short typography beats. The visual assets and UI are original, not copied from Notion or Slack. All motion depends on video time.

Narration and an original instrumental bed provide continuity. A different synthetic voice speaks for the Peer, with explicit AI identification. See [audio.md](audio.md) for final lines, synthesis provenance and mix details once generated.

## Illustrative fixtures

- Manish's role/team/projects come from the user brief; specific message content, dataset names, API names, timestamps and status values are invented demonstration data.
- Premium Chargebacks needs the dispute outcome before reconciliation. This is an illustrative requirement, not a statement of an actual internal spec.
- Sarah recommends the fictional `dispute_events` dataset via the fictional Events API. This demonstrates an owning human supplying an answer.
- The meeting reports development complete and validation in progress; launch confirmation is explicitly pending.
- Source labels demonstrate the desired evidence UI. They do not point to real company documents.
- No measured time savings are claimed. The story shows a dependency progressing while its owner is offline.
