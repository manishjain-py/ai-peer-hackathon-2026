## Keep statuses separate

“Designed,” “animated,” “rendered,” “prototype,” “connected,” and “tested” describe
different achievements. Do not change a product capability to implemented because
its scene appears in a finished MP4. Do not call a connector live because its logo
or source chip appears in the UI.
