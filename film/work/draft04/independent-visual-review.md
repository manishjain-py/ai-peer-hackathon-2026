## Creative assessment

The new opening communicates the user's circle idea substantially more clearly
than Draft 03. A seated employee is the visual center. The gaze moves toward the
active source; the smile becomes concern; the meeting beat raises a hand; the late
project-review demand produces an unmistakable unhappy expression. The email
count, growth to ten pings and three invites with two night times are legible.
The human and the cards remain visually distinct rather than becoming one busy
cluster.

The relief retains the same person and circle. At 22 seconds the old requests are
small and visibly moving toward Peer; at 25.5 seconds the employee is relaxed and
the front card holds the chosen work. “Routine questions” and “What matters to
you” communicate the two-way value. This is a connected response to the problem,
not an unrelated brand cut.

Setup makes the product understandable. The button is clear. The left source
panel changes from OneAirbnb profile to Sourcegraph code to GitHub work while the
right-hand personal understanding persists. Manish → Payments Platform →
Chargebacks → `event-mapper.ts` is a readable progression. The open checks and
saved handoff idea make the following office-hours and hackathon brief feel
grounded. Slack and Zoom controls show different uses; supporting email/company
events access explains the personal brief. Visible “illustrative setup” and
concept labels prevent these from looking like verified internal data.

Sampled retained scenes connect cleanly to the new setup. The same mapper appears
in the first brief, the two checks carry into Zoom, and the final decision remains
with Manish. No new major narrative or legibility blocker was found in these
samples.

## Requested fixes before final rendering

1. **Right-panel bottom chips extend below their container.** At 42.5, 45 and
   47.5 seconds, “Open checks” and “Saved handoff idea” cross the lower border of
   the green understanding panel by several pixels. Tighten the row spacing or
   move the chips upward so they have a proper bottom inset.
2. **Displayed Sourcegraph code should return the mapping.** The initial frames
   showed `outcome: event.outcome` in a bare function block, which reads as an
   unused JavaScript label. Use `return { outcome: event.outcome };` so the
   illustrative engineering evidence is coherent. The source already contained
   this correction when consulted after the feedback; a corrected still should
   confirm it fits.
3. **Optional emotional refinement:** the 150-email beat at 6.5 seconds still
   has nearly the initial smile. Flatten it or add a slight worried brow here so
   the inbox itself begins the stress, leaving the later meeting reaction room
   to escalate. This was sent to the character animator.

## Remaining creative limitations

The film is clear and polished but uses clean, restrained illustration rather than
photorealistic human footage or elaborate character acting. That is an appropriate
way to implement this opening; do not describe it as live footage. The setup still
uses separate product panels and a brief success screen rather than a fully
continuous camera move into the daily brief. This is a polish opportunity, not a
blocker to the user's requested story.

After the concrete fixes, inspect encoded motion around the ring release and
source-panel transitions to ensure they are smooth at normal speed. Stills cannot
establish perceived pacing, musical impact or natural-sounding speech.
