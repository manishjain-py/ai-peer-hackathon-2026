# Draft 02 — Keep the context moving

Revised 18 September 2026 following Manish’s feedback. Final editorial target:
**3:44.1, 1920×1080, 30 fps**. [Watch the finished Draft 02](../../renders/ai-peer-draft-02.mp4). The editable source script spans 240 seconds; seven
speech-free cuts shorten the Zoom dialogue by 15.9 seconds. The same edit map is
applied to picture, audio and standalone captions.

## Story and visual treatment

| Final time | Beat | Viewer takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:24 | A quick question becomes a Slack notification and travels toward Manish’s sleeping timezone. Waiting dots resolve into the Peer reveal. | Being online at different times makes a small question expensive. |
| 0:24–0:56 | Three approved project sources form Manish’s context. A newer hourly requirement replaces the old nightly one. Manish chooses sharing and meeting boundaries. | The Peer knows this employee’s work, retains sources, and has defined authority. |
| 0:56–1:59 | Realistic Slack reconstruction. Sarah asks daily totals versus per-dispute results; Alex notes it is 2 a.m. for Manish; the identified AI Peer supplies the use case and latest requirement. Sarah recommends the hourly source and shares field mapping. | A useful information dependency moves forward during the other team’s day. |
| 1:59–2:10 | The same project becomes a delegated Zoom calendar invitation. | The second scenario continues the first story. |
| 2:10–3:15.1 | Realistic Zoom reconstruction. Alex asks for Manish, Sarah notices he is absent, and the Peer identifies itself. It supplies Manish’s build status, two pending data checks, and the new Slack answer. Sarah offers test examples. The Peer leaves Friday’s launch decision to Manish. | This is useful participation with personal context, transparent AI identity, and a clear decision boundary. |
| 3:15.1–3:35.1 | Morning activity view connects Slack and Zoom evidence. Sarah’s examples are an agreed follow-up, not completed tests. | Manish returns to a reviewable handoff and one clear decision. |
| 3:35.1–3:44.1 | “Your work has a timezone. Your Peer doesn’t.” | Keep the context moving. |

The project is Chargebacks: a dashboard displaying whether a disputed payment was
won or lost. “Dashboard build complete” is intentionally distinct from integrated
or launched: the data source and validation remain part of the story.

## Creative choices

- Warm editorial typography, a moving question, contrasting day/night clocks,
  mint context paths, plum Slack chrome and blue Zoom chrome.
- Purposeful message reveals and automatic scrolling, exact spoken-word caption
  timing, active speaker outlines, and a smooth gallery-to-screen-share transition.
- Fictional photoreal webcam stills and matching Slack avatars; these are not
  lip-synced video. The Peer remains a graphic AI participant, not Manish’s likeness.
- Distinct stock voices for narrator, Sarah, Alex and Peer. Original synthesized
  music changes with the scene and leaves space around the meeting dialogue.
- Persistent concept label plus a readable closing disclosure that integrations
  are not implemented. App interfaces, participants and all project evidence are
  authored illustrative reconstructions, not live product recordings.

## Independent review and refinement

The independent script reviewer corrected the premature “integration complete”
claim, made Sarah’s hourly-source confirmation explicit, and simplified the two
validation checks. The first visual review required better Slack scrolling,
opening movement, an actual source preview, consistent channel naming and Peer
mark, and shorter gaps in the meeting. These refinements were incorporated.
