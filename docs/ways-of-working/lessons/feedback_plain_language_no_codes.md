Manish does NOT hold the internal shorthand the design/working docs use — phase labels (E1, E2, E3, E4…), decision numbers (E9, E10, E13), tracker item numbers (#22, #23). He made the underlying decisions in plain conversation; the docs encoded them as codes afterward. Echoing those codes back at him in chat is confusing and reads as jargon.

**Why:** He said directly — "you keep using terms like E4, E13 etc. i have no idea what they mean" (session impact-1, 2026-06-13).

**How to apply:** In conversation, say the thing in plain language and only use a code if you define it inline the first time. Examples: "the first chapter batch — make decks for the 3 new Grade-4 Ch1 topics" instead of "E4"; "your earlier call not to auto-replace live decks yet, because the rebuild is expensive" instead of "E13". Codes are fine *inside* the working docs themselves — just never make him decode them in chat. Reinforces [[feedback_concise_docs]] and the global simple-language rule.

**Added 2026-09-10 (explain-group-a-2 session):** when walking review findings one at a time, he asked
that each finding be explained "in simplified manner and in short" — a few plain sentences: what is
wrong, what it breaks, what the fix is, then the ask. Facts and sweep evidence stay, but compressed
(a table or two lines, not a report). He decides fast; a long write-up slows him down.

**Added 2026-10-31 (codex-factories-controller-31):** made general for every ask: "Wherever you need my reply, first
explain the point to me in simplified manner." So any question, step or approval that needs his reply opens with the
point in plain words (what it is about, why his reply is needed, what happens after each answer), then the ask.
