When a brief or relayed question says "the controller" (or any other session) asked or decides something, name that session: its session name, Terminal window id and remote-control link (the build's tracker usually has them; in learnlikemagic, STATE.md's session table), and say which session you are.

**Why:** On 2026-09-26 a catch-me-up said "the controller sent you a question"; Manish replied "where's the controller session? what is its name? are you the controller session?" He drives many sessions from his phone and cannot act on an unnamed one.

**How to apply:** In catch-me-up briefs and in any question put to him on another session's behalf, write e.g. "the controller, `docs-cleanup-ctl2` (window 35534, <remote link>)", and name yourself as a worker when you are one. The companion rule, launching every controller as `<feature>-controller-<n>`, is [[feedback_controller_session_names]].
