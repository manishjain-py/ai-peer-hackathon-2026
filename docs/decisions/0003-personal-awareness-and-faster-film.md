# 0003 — Personal awareness and a faster film

Date: 2026-09-18. Builds on [Draft 02](0002-realistic-conversations.md).

## User direction

Manish found Draft 02 too slow and its meeting voices unnatural. He requested
faster pacing, more conversational voices, upbeat music synchronized with
transitions, a clearer problem and product reveal, a stronger tagline and more
use cases. Zoom should address the visible Peer directly; only Slack keeps the
2 a.m. explanation. He explicitly requested delivery of the next film to his Telegram.

His subsequent clarification changes the central product framing: AI Peer is
also for the employee's own awareness and effectiveness. It should keep track
of permitted email, Slack and company events, connect changes to the employee's
services, projects and interests, and provide a personal brief with useful
priorities, events and opportunities.

## Decision

Draft 03 centers a forty-second personal-brief sequence before the collaboration
scenarios. A service migration becomes an affected-code review; relevant office
hours become a prepared question; a fitting hackathon becomes a draft pitch.
The same work context then supports Slack and Zoom. The next personal brief
incorporates their results while preserving the decision that needs Manish.

The working film tagline is **“Your work. A step ahead.”** It accommodates
proactive personal help as well as delegated collaboration. It is a creative
choice for this draft, not a permanently approved product name or slogan.

## Production changes

- Final target 166.5 seconds (2:46.5), versus Draft 02's 224.1 seconds.
- New local Qwen3 synthesis with text-directed performances and separately
  designed fictional female voices, replacing the previous Edge TTS engine.
- Original upbeat 124 BPM score, with each scene transition starting on a
  musical downbeat and speech-aware music reduction.
- Direct address to the identified Peer in Zoom and 350ms gaps between its turns.
- Camera-off human participant tiles avoid presenting frozen portraits as live video.
- Authored sources flow through personal relevance into prepared actions. The
  same brief workspace returns with new information and priorities.

## Boundaries

All source messages, company events, code references, employees and outcomes are
illustrative. No continuous monitoring or live integration was implemented.
Preparing a question or pitch is distinct from sending, registering or committing.
Launch approval stays with Manish. Direct listening cannot be claimed from ASR
or audio-level measurements. Earlier drafts and the original idea remain preserved.
