## Plan the performance before generating or rendering

Save an acting map in the repository with exact movie/source times, the scene/context,
Moss's intention, gaze/expression, physical activity, the transition/hold, and required assets.
Read the actual timeline and narration; do not invent new dialogue or alter product meaning.
Use varied activity where the context changes, with calm holds so the audience can read the UI.
The controller makes routine acting choices and checks them in context; an intermediate user
approval round is not required.

Suggested beats to investigate (the actual timing comes from the film):

- Arrival/setup: alert curiosity, a brief welcome, then attention to the task.
- Profile and source reading: look toward the active source, scan, write a short note in a
  small notebook, look back to the source; avoid a continuous greeting wave.
- Consolidating/learned context: review notes, think, then a small recognition or acknowledgment.
- Brief: organize or select points and guide attention to the relevant card.
- Ask: attentive listening while the question is entered, a real thinking/search pause, then
  a grounded, reassuring presentation when the answer and its sources arrive.
- Rules: calm reassurance and deliberate attention to the boundary; keep the human in control.
- Zoom: listen to colleagues, present the update, think at a question, and note the question for
  Ram when the existing line defers a decision. Do not gesture as though Moss made that decision.
- Slack: attentive response activity only where it is visible and useful; small brand marks
  need not be constantly animated.
- Morning handoff: gently guide attention to what needs Ram, then acknowledge his actual click.
- Return/ending: a relaxed, satisfied handoff and calm finish. Preserve the phone origin/return.
