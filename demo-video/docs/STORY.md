# The story (proposed by ai-peer-video-controller-1, 2026-11-14; not yet approved by Manish)

Working title: **"He's asleep. I'm not."** Two minutes, told by the Peer itself, in the first person.

## Why this story

The September film (Draft 07, 4:23) explains: a scattered workday, a setup wizard, four use
cases on drawn screens, a daily brief. It informs, but it has no single picture to remember and
no feeling. The new film keeps its three best use cases (the cross-team meeting, the answer
across time zones, the heads-up about a change elsewhere) and shows them on the real product.
Around them it puts one idea everybody has had, "I wish there were two of me", one picture (the
mint double that rises while he sleeps), and one voice (the Peer, honest about being an AI).

## The five parts

Now someone's still there." | Code only (a 3D Earth) |

End card: the AI Peer mark, the line "More room for your best work.", and "Built for real this
week".

## What makes it honest

- The double is a picture of the idea, and it is tagged "AI Peer" on screen.
- The Peer says "Not as him. As me.", speaks in its own voice (never his), and the real product
  shows the same label.
- It never decides for him: the meeting beat and the morning both show decisions going back to
  him.
- The hook does not trick the colleagues: one of them says the answer came from his Peer.

## What it costs

About nine AI shots with people (three colleagues, the sleeper, the rise, the walk and sit,
the wake, the brief, the nod), about 45 seconds of footage. Everything else is code: the
effects on the double, the product screens, the clock, the Earth, the titles, the edit. The
estimate is $50 to $60 on top of WP1's test round, inside the $100 cap.
