## The story in five lines

1. A good workday gets buried under messages, mail and meetings.
2. An AI teammate can take that load.
3. Setup is one click, your existing work context, and access you choose.
4. It spots risks, answers while you sleep, attends meetings for you and catches what you
   missed, always labelled as AI.
5. These are only examples; the gain is room for your best work.
