# Scoring the approved Moss film

The main listening question is whether the groove is audible at the same playback volume throughout the walkthrough, including when someone speaks. The original stem report shows a 22.4 dB voice lead on “Setting me up takes one click” and 21.4 dB on both the sourced-facts line and Ram’s morning decision. Those figures describe the stems used by the v6 mixer. The approved v7 uses that soundtrack. They help locate the quiet spots but do not measure how a listener hears a consonant, guitar or drum on a phone. [Original stem measurements](../moss-clarity/audio/mix-report.json)

## Research and editorial choices

- **Choose a few meaningful musical arrivals.** Sean McMahon’s Berklee scoring notes describe matching energy to picture movement, dialogue and section changes, and fitting phrases between selected sync points. For this film the strongest choices are Moss’s emergence, the first sustained walkthrough groove, the change into the meeting, the reveal of the brief and the final cadence. Accenting every UI click would compete with the story. [Berklee scoring guide](https://online.berklee.edu/takenote/5-film-scoring-concepts-to-know-for-your-first-movie-score/)
- **Give the voice space without losing the pulse.** Adobe distinguishes the amount of ducking from its timing and sensitivity. iZotope describes making room in the presence range of the voice, including a selective dip around 2–5 kHz. For these auditions, use a restrained change in the music under sentences, keep a rhythmic part audible and let phrase ends recover. The suitable depth depends on the arrangement. [Adobe on ducking controls](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/add-audio-effects/adjust-volume-and-levels/automatically-duck-audio.html) · [iZotope on mixing under dialogue](https://www.izotope.com/community/blog/6-tips-for-mixing-in-surround)
- **Make musical alternatives distinct.** DeepMind’s music guide identifies instrumentation, tempo, genre and sectional dynamics as separately steerable choices. Five new instrumental briefs contrast acoustic picking, a live funk pocket, an electronic pulse, pizzicato chamber writing and a syncopated samba-jazz rhythm. Each has a different way to suggest optimism while leaving room for the narrator and meeting voices. [Lyria prompt guide](https://deepmind.google/models/lyria/prompt-guide/)
- **Compare at matched loudness.** EBU R128 describes integrated programme loudness and true peak as separate measurements. Use both on the actual exported AAC, and inspect individual scenes as well. For this audition the playback reference is the approved Telegram soundtrack, approximately −14 LUFS, rather than a broadcast delivery target. Similar overall levels make meaningful musical differences easier to hear. [EBU R128](https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/r/r128.pdf) · [Short-form guidance](https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r128s1)

## Cue map for listening

| Film time | Picture and sound to compare at one device volume |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Three short human hooks. A light but recognizable pulse should carry the spaces between replies. |
| 0:12–0:24 | The phone lights up and Moss appears. Listen for an arrival and a lift into setup while the chime and voice stay clear. |
| 0:24–0:59 | Source reading, the brief, Ask and the confidence stamp. A persistent groove should make the long run of narration feel continuous. The short green-board reading interval gives the score space to come forward. |
| 0:59–1:19 | Zoom has alternating voices and a human decision. A leaner arrangement suits the meeting, but its pulse should still register. |
| 1:19–1:40 | Typing leads into the company stream. Build momentum over the three catches and let the completed brief register. |
| 1:40–1:51 | Wake, laptop and “Needs you”. Keep warmth and rhythmic continuity; the listener must be able to follow the decision line at the same volume as setup. |
| 1:51–1:59.6 | Globe, last sentence and end card. Look for an intentional musical landing after the words, with enough room for a natural tail. |

A is the control for the original composition with revised balance. B–F test five different arrangements against the same picture, dialogue and sound effects. The release record for the approved film is [Moss v7 approval](../docs/MOSS-V7-APPROVAL.md).
