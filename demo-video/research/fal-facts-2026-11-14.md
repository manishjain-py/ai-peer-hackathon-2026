# fal.ai facts for the AI shots, and corrections to WP3's brief

Read on 2026-11-14 between 11:50 and 12:05 UTC by a read-only helper of
ai-peer-video-controller-2, with free calls only (the pricing API, each model's
`https://fal.ai/models/<id>/llms.txt` page and its schema). The controller re-read three of
them itself (Kling's default resolution and its two prices; the matte tool's download folder;
the size of `f1.png`). Nothing here was tested with a paid call. WP3's brief
(`docs/handoffs/HANDOFF-2026-11-14-ai-peer-video-wp3.md`) is never edited: this file corrects it.

## Corrections to the brief

1. **Always set `resolution`.** The pricing API returns only the cheapest tier.
   `kling/v4/image-to-video` costs $0.157 a second at 720p and $0.235 at 1080p, and its default
   is 1080p. Omni's listed price is its 360p rate and its default is 720p (three times that).
   The price per resolution and the inputs are on `https://fal.ai/models/<id>/llms.txt` (free,
   no key).
2. **Kling and Omni take no `seed`,** so a winning 720p take cannot be made again at 1080p. Make
   takes at 720p and upscale only the winner (table 3).
3. **The stills are not 16:9** (`f1.png` is 2752x1536; Kling returned 1280x716, MiniMax
   1344x768). Deliver by scale-to-cover and a centre crop, never a stretch. Measure `tag_track`
   and `screen_quad` on the delivered frame, and crop `last_still` the same way.
4. **Reference pictures cannot go into image-to-video on routes O and K:** those calls hold only
   a first frame, middle key frames (Kling) and a last frame. Give the man's reference pictures
   to the still calls. For video, `kling/v4/reference-to-video` takes `image_urls` (up to 10)
   plus key frames, at the same price.
5. **The two lip-sync endpoints the brief names need longer sound than our lines** (2.6, 2.1 and
   1.1 s): `minimax/h3-max/lip-sync/image-to-video` needs at least 5 s and costs $0.08 a second
   at 768P (not $0.05), `bytedance/omnihuman/v2` needs 4 to 300 s. They are fallbacks at about
   $0.40 a shot with the sound padded. Cheaper candidates are in table 2.
6. **Every model returns its own sound and none can turn it off.** Strip it (`-an`) for a slot
   marked `audio: none`.
7. **`reveal-rise` may be two clips joined at a still.** Rise and stand filled WP1's whole 10 s,
   and Omni gives at most 10 s with an end frame. The camera is locked, so a join at the
   standing still is invisible: rise and stand, then the blanket, delivered as one file.
8. **The ledger can undercount** when the live price cannot be read (the pricing API answered
   "Rate limit exceeded" in a burst of 12 calls): look for "could not be read" in the tool's
   output and re-price those rows from the billed units in the `.result.json`.
9. **`person_matte.py` downloads its 120 MB model into the folder it is run from,** and a
   worker's shell starts in the shared checkout: run it from the scratchpad (`cd` in the same
   command). It mattes every person in the picture, the sleeper too.
10. **Paid calls only through `falgen.py`** (WP3: `gen-wp3.sh`). The Gemini, OpenAI and Stability
    keys on this Mac belong to other work: never spend on them.
11. **Money notes for the controller:** every milestone message carries the ledger total, and
    WP3 tells the controller when it passes $30 (Manish is told at $50 and at $80 of the whole
    $100, `DESIGN.md` C5).
12. **Order of the finals, changed by the controller:** the shots without the double first
    (`hook-sleeper`, `hook-1` to `hook-3`, `morning-wake`, `morning-brief`), then the three with
    the double (`reveal-rise`, `reveal-desk`, `morning-nod`), so Manish can see the sheet of
    looks for the double before the dearest shots are made. If his answer has not come when the
    others are done, WP3 goes on with its own choice.
